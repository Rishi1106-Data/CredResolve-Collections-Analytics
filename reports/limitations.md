# Limitations and unresolved issues

1. **Coverage is 1 Jan – 8 Aug 2026 (≈7.2 months), not 12.** Month-on-month comparisons exist for Feb–Jul only; August is partial.
2. **The reported "11%" is not defined.** The only month pair that matches is Feb→Mar total SUCCESS recovery (+11.03%). Other definitions cannot be tested.
3. **Statistical tests are exploratory.** 212 daily values from one synthetic extract; several tests without multiplicity control. Daily values may be serially correlated: autocorrelation diagnostics (Ljung–Box p=0.20 / 0.32) do not detect it, but power is limited, so robust versions (Newey–West slope, 7-day block bootstrap) are quoted. A non-significant result means **no statistically detectable trend was identified in the observed period**; it does not show performance is unchanged. The per-day 95% interval (−5.2% to +5.9%) shows changes of that size cannot be excluded.
4. **No eligible-balance denominator.** `accounts.status`, `dpd` and `outstanding_amount` are single snapshot columns; 75% of accounts are PAID/CLOSED/WRITEOFF yet receive 75% of recovery at the same rate as ACTIVE; `account_status_history` agrees with `accounts.status` for only 14.2% of accounts; 13.8% of paying accounts recovered more than their snapshot balance. "Recovery ÷ outstanding" is therefore an index, not a collection rate (`reports/denominator_audit.md`). The terminal-status share is steady at ≈43% month to month, so we see no sign of accounts leaving the population, but that is not proof about the source reporting.
5. **PTP kept rate is a range, not a measurement.** Payments carry no promise ID, so a payment in the window is only *consistent with* a kept promise. Only 8 payments fall in two or more windows of the same account (16 of 16,758 matured promises), so double-counting is small here. Payment-checked kept rate: 3.67% (one-to-one) to 3.71% (overlap allowed); recorded KEPT is 24.94% of matured promises (49.76% of KEPT+BROKEN) and is not supported by payments. Promises created in Jul/Aug are maturity-censored.
6. **Borrower entity resolution failed.** 8,518 `borrower_id`s have several different name/city/state rows; 15,063 rows have `updated_at` < `created_at`; `borrower_id` disagrees across tables. Unique-borrower metrics and geography are not reported.
7. **Agent entity resolution failed.** One `agent_id` carries ~29 employee codes/names/teams. Agent tenure, productivity and recovery per agent-hour are not computed; 1,759 calls (1,827 raw rows) have no agent.
8. **Timezones.** Payments and PTPs have no timezone. Calls carry a label, but treating `event_at` as local time in that label is an assumption; under it 13.2% of calls change calendar date and 66.7% change hour, so hour-of-day findings are provisional.
9. **No language field** in any table.
10. **No cost data and no keep rate**: cost per ₹ recovered, ROI and break-even cannot be measured. Only hypothetical sensitivities are shown and labelled as such.
11. **Payments are not linked to campaigns or interactions.** Last-call attribution performs no better than a placebo, so recovery by channel/campaign is not identifiable.
12. **Disposition data is unreliable** (~80% of dispositions on unanswered calls; PTP/PROMISE_TO_PAY aliases; versions spread evenly over months), so no mapping change is detectable.
13. **Experiment baseline is observational.** The 3.76% 14-day payment rate (22,736 unique targeted accounts with an observable window) sizes a future test; it does not measure any treatment effect. The earlier row-level 3.59% / 11,557-per-arm figure is withdrawn (rows repeat accounts).
14. **Counterfactual not estimable.** No identifiable strategy change date (campaign `strategy_version` dates overlap Jan–May) and no assignment log.
15. **Multiple testing.** 18 driver screens were run; 1 showed p<0.05 (DPD bucket vs paid (p=0.028)), not significant after Bonferroni correction. About 0.9 false positives are expected at 5%; the Bonferroni threshold is 0.0028.
16. **WhatsApp, SMS and field tables** were screened (first event per account vs 14-day payment) but not fully cleaned (WhatsApp has 600 exact duplicate rows). `complaints` and `agent_sessions` were not analysed.
17. **The dataset is synthetic** (README says so; category counts almost uniform). The absence of effects may reflect how it was generated, not business reality.
